# Medicare-plus
A modern online pharmacy a# MediCare Plus - Online Pharmacy & Healthcare Platform 🏥💊

MediCare Plus is a comprehensive and responsive web-based online pharmacy and telehealth dashboard built with React. It allows users to browse medicines, manage a shopping cart, upload prescriptions with built-in QR scanning, and book doctor consultations.

Developed with ❤️ by **Prince Alam**

---

## ✨ Features

- 🛒 **Interactive Product Catalog:** Filter medicines by category (Medicines, Vitamins, Baby Care, etc.) or search in real time.
- 🛍️ **Cart & Checkout System:** Dynamic cart calculations, subtotal, delivery charges, and simulated order checkout.
- 📱 **Prescription Upload & QR Scanner:** Option to upload prescription images or scan medical QR codes directly via camera.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Consultation:** In-built form to schedule doctor appointments with complaints and preferred time slots.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive UI:** Clean design with custom CSS variables, smooth animations, and mobile-friendly layout.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React 18 (CDN), Babel Standalone
- **Styling:** CSS3 (Custom Properties & Flexbox/Grid)
- **Libraries:** `html5-qrcode` (Camera-based QR reader)
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (Crimson Pro & DM Sans)
